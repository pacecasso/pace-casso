# PaceCasso — handoff for the next engineer or AI (2026-09-05)

Written by the AI that worked this project through September 4, 2026, at the
owner's decision to bring in someone new. Everything below is checkable from
files in this repo. Verify it; don't trust it. Read
`INDEPENDENT-AUDIT-2026-09-04.md` first — it is a fresh agent's view of the
live site as a paying user, and its verdict ("would not pay today") stands.

## What the product is supposed to do

Upload a logo → get a runnable NYC route that a stranger recognizes as that
logo, automatically, at the quality of the reference GPS art in the repo root
(`lion.webp`, `TIGER.webp`, `sneaker.jpg`, `HEART.webp`). The owner's stated
acceptance test since August 21: `gas.png` (fuel pump + person with
headphones, blue on a yellow disc) comes back recognizable. His calibration
on the likeness judge: 7 = "awful", 8 = "better". Nothing has reached a
reliable 8.

## What works (verified)

- The live site (`www.pacecasso.com`, Vercel, auto-deploys from `main`)
  runs end to end for Manhattan: upload → trace → one-button search →
  editor → GPX/GeoJSON/cues export. Simple bold silhouettes (heart, apple,
  dog, star) come back recognizable; the heart scores 8/8/8 on the likeness
  judge in about a minute.
- `lib/strokePainter.ts` — the current drawing primitive: filled silhouette →
  mass (outline rings + sparse hatch) and thin residue (centerlines), seated
  only where the street grid is uniform, vertices snapped to measured street
  lines, routed on the cached walk graph with no Mapbox and no model calls.
  `app/api/paint-route` serves it in ~2–8 s as the "first draft" that every
  search now starts with. Unit test: `lib/strokePainter.test.ts`.
- `scripts/gas-composition.ts` — research rig for multi-part logos: splits
  bodies and links, seats each body on its own grid (Manhattan, Astoria,
  Jackson Heights, central Brooklyn), draws the hose between them, ranks
  candidates with the blind likeness judge. Needs the merged graph
  (`node scripts/build-nyc-core-graph.mjs`, inputs in gitignored `tmp-wow/`
  and `tmp-city-osm/`; re-fetch with `scripts/fetch-bounds-osm.mjs` +
  `scripts/build-packed-walk-graph.mjs`).

## What does not work (measured, not guessed)

- **The gas logo.** Best automatic results: likeness 7/8/7 (East Flatbush,
  figure drawn 1.3×, 50 km) and 6/7/8 (Astoria pump + Jackson Heights
  figure, 41 km); GPX for both in the repo root (`gas-best-*.gpx`). Judges
  who are NOT shown the logo read every version as "person walking a dog"
  or "person flying a kite". Their recurring reason: pump, hose and person
  recognizable, figure distorted, headphones unclear. Tried: Manhattan,
  Astoria, Jackson Heights, Brooklyn; scales 1200–4400 m; figure at 1.0–1.5×;
  stacked layout; hose as logo curve / arc / walk chain; vertex snapping
  and whole-block rasterization; a badge-disc ring (would not route).
- **Every earlier method** (July–August: lattice compiler, organic trace,
  landmark composition, LLM redraw loops, lockups) plateaued at 4–7 on the
  same logo. See `STATE-OF-PROJECT.md` (July 23) and the memory notes in
  the commit messages.
- **Numeric proxies do not predict recognition.** `CALIBRATION.md`: the
  shape-match score's correlation with blind recognition is 0.14. My own
  fidelity number in the composition rig was equally useless. Only the blind
  judge (`scripts/blind-squint-test.mjs` protocol) predicts what people see.
- **Only Manhattan works in production.** The city picker offers five.

## Where the ceiling is, in my judgment

Block resolution. NYC blocks are ~80 × 200–270 m. A figure 1.2–1.7 km wide
keeps its silhouette and loses its face. Drawing it larger needs >4 km of one
uniform grid, which exists only in central Brooklyn, where the pump routing
turned flaky. The reference pieces get their detail from size (the lion spans
~14 km and two boroughs) and from hand-chosen streets. Whether an automatic
system can match a human's stroke choices at that size is the open question;
I did not answer it.

## Things I would not repeat

- Presenting near-misses or diagnostics as progress. The owner has been
  burned by this repeatedly (see the Aug 12 notes referenced in commits).
- Calling anything "verified" that a blind judge did not name. The
  `verified`/`draft` flags on routes exist for this reason (`lib/routeTypes.ts`).
- Trusting internal scores. Judge every candidate blind, on the real map.
- Simplifying the input. The owner was right that the reference art has MORE
  detail than our output; the missing ingredients are size and deliberate
  strokes, not fewer of them.
